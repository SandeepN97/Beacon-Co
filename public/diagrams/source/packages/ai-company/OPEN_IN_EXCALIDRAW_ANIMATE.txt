ONE-FILE EXCALIDRAW ANIMATE PACKAGE

RECOMMENDED: LOAD THE LIBRARY
1. Open https://dai-shi.github.io/excalidraw-animate/
2. Click "Load Library".
3. Choose: ai_company_all_agents_and_combined.excalidrawlib
4. The library contains:
   - Combined end-to-end business + broker architecture
   - Full 14-agent company board
   - 14 separate individual agent architecture diagrams
5. Select/animate the library items.

ALTERNATIVE: LOAD ONE LARGE CANVAS
1. Click "Load File".
2. Choose: ai_company_all_agents_and_combined_canvas.excalidraw
3. This places every diagram on one large editable canvas.

WHY THERE ARE TWO FORMATS
- .excalidrawlib is easier to view one diagram at a time.
- .excalidraw places everything on one continuous canvas.

A permanent public one-click URL requires the drawing file to be published
to a public host or uploaded as an Excalidraw share link. This package keeps
your architecture private and local.
