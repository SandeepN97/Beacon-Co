## Interpretation rules

- Proposal claims are evidence until an accepted decision adopts them.
- “Accepted” is not the same as implemented.
- Vendor choices must be revalidated when their phase begins.
- Named prospects are research leads, not clients or endorsements.
- Missing editable Excalidraw files must not be claimed as present.
- The preserved HTML remains evidence; the Decision Book controls project interpretation.
