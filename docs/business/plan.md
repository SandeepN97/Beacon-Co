# Business Plan — Current Reference

[ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-why-beacon-exists-and-business-definition.md) is the complete source of truth for the origin, market evidence, 33 visible prospects, competitor dossiers, offer, economics, retention, roadmap, and risks. This page is the short operating reference.

## Concept

Beacon & Co. is a solo-operated digital-presence business for locally owned small businesses within roughly 30 miles of Waynesboro, Virginia.

It began with a local observation: many good businesses had no credible site, an incomplete Google listing, or a social page standing in for an owned presence. Beacon starts with a useful free audit, recommends the smallest fitting service, and earns the recurring relationship through visible results.

## Customer

- credible in the real world but incomplete online;
- locally owned and within the operating radius;
- values founder access and plain language;
- cannot justify a traditional agency retainer;
- wants to own its files and understand what changed.

## Operating loop

```text
Local observation or referral
  → useful free audit
  → founder sales conversation
  → smallest fitting paid product
  → visible result
  → optional recurring relationship
```

Current delivery is founder-led. Automation may eventually prepare research, audits, content, media, publishing, and reports, but the founder retains the sales decision and the client approves drafted content before publication.

## Economics boundary

The proposal’s ~93% gross-margin claim, scenarios, churn assumptions, LTV:CAC, capacity, vendor costs, and growth curves are hypotheses. They are preserved with their formulas in [ADR-0001](../decisions/0001-why-beacon-exists-and-business-definition.md#financial-model), not reported as measured results.

Track before treating the model as validated:

- acquisition cost and conversion;
- founder sales and delivery time;
- software and provider cost per client;
- revisions, support, refunds, and failed payments;
- gross margin by offer;
- retention and expansion.

## Open decisions

- formal name, domain, and trademark clearance;
- proof that the public prices support actual delivery time;
- verified local-market and competitor data;
- the first recurring workflow worth automating.
